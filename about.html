About Save Wave

A simpler way to access video download options.

Save Wave provides a straightforward interface for checking available download options from supported video links.

Designed for simplicity

Paste a supported video link, review the options returned by the downloader service, and choose how to proceed. The website is designed for use on phones and computers.

Use responsibly

Only download content you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally allowed to save. Respect copyright, privacy, and the rules of the original platforms.

Third-party services

Save Wave relies on a separately hosted downloader service. Availability and supported links may change, and the service may sometimes be unavailable.
